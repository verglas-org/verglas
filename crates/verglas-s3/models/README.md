the Apache-2.0-licensed [boto/botocore](https://github.com/boto/botocore)
repository (`botocore/data/s3vectors/2025-07-15/service-2.json`). Its original
copyright and license remain with Amazon.com, Inc. and the botocore
contributors.

`verglas-graphs-service-1.json` is the Verglas Graph REST-JSON contract and is
covered by this repository's license.
